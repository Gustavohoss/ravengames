Jogos interativos no TikTok são 100% permitidos e incentivados pela plataforma, desde que o envio de presentes sirva exclusivamente como gatilho de entretenimento visual na tela. O algoritmo pune apenas quem promete dinheiro de volta aos doadores (rifas ou apostas) ou transmite telas estáticas.

What Does TikTok's Integrity Policy Say About Virtual Gifts?

TikTok LIVE's economic model is based on the circulation of Virtual Goods (Virtual Items & Gifts). For each gift that a viewer buys to send on live, TikTok retains a percentage of the currency and passes the diamonds on to the creator of the broadcast.

Therefore, TikTok actively wants streams to generate real incentives for viewers to send gifts. What the official Community Guidelines document (Section: Integrity and Authenticity) expressly prohibits is the following conduct:

  • Prohibition of Gambling and Raffles (Gambling & Lotteries): Any explicit promise that whoever sends a Rose will participate in a Pix draw or receive cash prizes is considered a misdemeanor and will result in immediate termination of the live.
  • Prohibition of Engagement Fraud: Encouraging the sending of gifts by claiming false emergencies or promises that will not be fulfilled.
  • Prohibition of Inauthentic Content: Trying to simulate live presence through pre-recorded videos (the famous "loop video").

Games like Flappy Bird from Live, Tower Sky or Battle of the Nicks from Roblox from Raven Games fall under Gamified Viewer Interaction. The gift is a playful currency used to interact with the game's 3D environment — exactly how arcade games have worked for decades.

The 4 Golden Rules for Never Taking a Strike or Suspension

By strictly following these four simple rules established by the Raven Games compliance team, your account will operate with a 100% security rate, streaming day and night without any interruption:

Regra de Ouro #1 • Risco Crítico

Never Promise Financial Returns to Donors

Never put phrases like: "Whoever sends Galáxia wins a R$50 Pix" or "Top 1 donor wins an iPhone" in the title or banners on the screen. This practice constitutes a draw not regulated by the Ministry of Finance and violates TikTok's global terms.

How to do it right: Let entertainment be the prize! The viewer sends the gift to see their avatar dance on the screen, knock down their rival's bird or climb onto the game's scoring podium.

Regra de Ouro #2 • Algoritmo Anti-Queda

Keep the Screen Constantly Moving (Anti-Idle)

TikTok's artificial intelligence system monitors broadcast pixels. If the game remains static without visual changes for more than 5 to 10 minutes (for example, in the early hours of the morning when the chat slows down), the live receives a "Broadcast Inactive" warning.

The Raven Games Solution: Our software has a built-in Anti-Idle Engine. When the comments decrease, the game itself triggers autonomous animations, sound challenges and programmed events to keep the TikTok algorithm active and recommending your live in the For You tab.

Regra de Ouro #3 • Renderização Nativa em Tempo Real

Stream Native Games at 60 FPS (Zero Video Recorded)

TikTok can easily identify broadcasts that play video files (MP4) in a continuous loop by analyzing the metadata of the video frames.

Raven Games games are native executable applications that render each frame in real time on your computer's GPU. There are no recorded files; physics, obstacles and avatars respond instantly to the impulses of every second of the broadcast.

Regra de Ouro #4 • Moderação de Nomes e Palavras Proibidas

Filter Swear Words and Toxic Chat Terms

In games like Roblox Avatars, where the viewer's username appears printed on the game screen, malicious users can create accounts with offensive nicknames to try to take down your live.

How to protect yourself: In your TikTok LIVE Studio dashboard and Raven Games dashboard, enable the blocklist for sensitive keywords (hateful terms, explicit content, or violence). The software automatically rules out the display of inappropriate nicknames.

TikTok Guidelines Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Does TikTok charge commission on gifts the interactive game receives?

Yes. As with any live, TikTok keeps approximately 50% of the gross value of the gift and credits the other 50% in liquid diamonds to your creator wallet, which you withdraw via Pix.

2. Can I leave the interactive live stream running in the early hours of the morning while I sleep?

Yes! As long as you use software with anti-idle protection like Raven Games and keep the chat moderated, the live will continue to operate and monetize without the need for human intervention.

3. I have already been warned in the past on another live. Can I start now with Raven Games?

Yes. Warnings gradually expire after 90 days under TikTok's guidelines. By migrating to Raven Games' secure framework, your stream operates under full technical compliance.
