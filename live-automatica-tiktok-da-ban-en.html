What Does the TikTok Algorithm Really Punish in Lives?

TikTok is extremely strict on live streams, but most punishments occur for very specific reasons that have nothing to do with interactive games:

  • Video Recorded in Loop Pretending to Be Live: People who play an MP4 on infinite repeat to try to deceive viewers are immediately banned for "inauthentic content".
  • Total Screen Inactivity: Leaving the camera pointed at a wall or a frozen screen for more than 10 minutes causes the algorithm to suspend the live stream.
  • Hacker DLL Injection: Illegal software that tries to alter TikTok's encrypted packages violates the social network's cybersecurity.
