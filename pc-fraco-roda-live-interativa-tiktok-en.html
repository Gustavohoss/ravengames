The Myth of the “R$5,000 PC Gamer” for Broadcasting

For years, the streaming industry spread the idea that to broadcast live it was strictly necessary to have a top-of-the-line computer with an i7/i9 processor, 32GB of RAM and a liquid-cooled RTX graphics card.

If your goal was to run Cyberpunk 2077 or Warzone in 4K while streaming, this would be true. However, in TikTok's interactive lives market, the game broadcast is a minigame focused on vertical engagement (1080x1920) and direct physics.

With the right engineering, this type of game can run as smoothly as a fast-moving web page, allowing any office machine to become a profitable broadcast station.

The Secret of Lightness: Raven Games vs. Heavy Android Emulators

The reason most people have bad experiences when trying to do interactive lives on their own is the use of emulators like BlueStacks, LDPlayer, Nox or Memu.

When you run an Android emulator on your Windows:

  • The computer is forced to virtualize an entire Android operating system within Windows;
  • The emulator consumes 3GB to 6GB of RAM just to initialize the interface;
  • The CPU is stuck between 70% and 95% of use, causing crashes, overheating and dropped frames in live.

Raven Games took the opposite path: all of our interactive games (like Flappy Bird, Tower Sky 3D and Avatar Battles) were programmed as compiled native applications.

4 Tips to Ensure Smooth 60 FPS on a Basic Notebook

1. Configure o OBS com Resolução Nativa Vertical:

Set the OBS base and output screen to 1080x1920. Since Raven Games' minigames are already natively rendered vertically, your graphics card doesn't spend processing cycles resizing or cropping the image.

2. Utilize o Codificador de Hardware Correto:

If your CPU has an Intel integrated card, select the QuickSync H.264 encoder in the OBS output settings. If you have a dedicated card, select NVENC (Nvidia) or AMF (AMD). This offloads processing from the central processor.

3. Ajuste a Taxa de Bits (Bitrate):

For TikTok broadcasts, a bitrate between 2,500 Kbps and 3,500 Kbps is the perfect balance between crystal clear clarity and low bandwidth and CPU consumption.

4. Conecte o Notebook Sempre na Tomada:

Modern notebooks go into power saving mode when operating on battery power, reducing the CPU clock by half. Keep the charger plugged in and select the 'High Performance' power plan in Windows.
