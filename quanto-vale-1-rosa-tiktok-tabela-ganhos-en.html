Understanding the Math Behind TikTok Gifts

Anyone watching TikTok Live streams frequently sees an avalanche of roses, donuts, galaxies, and lions flying across the screen. But how do these virtual tokens translate into real money in the creator's pocket?

The financial engine of TikTok Live operates through a transparent three-step cycle:

  1. Coins (Purchased by the Viewer): Viewers purchase coin packages inside the app. Each coin costs approximately $0.012 to $0.015 depending on package size.
  2. Diamonds (Credited to the Streamer): When a viewer sends a gift during the stream, TikTok deducts its standard 50% platform service fee and deposits the remaining 50% as "Diamonds" in the streamer's creator wallet.
  3. Cash Withdrawal: Each accumulated diamond has a guaranteed fixed payout value of $0.005. Creators can withdraw directly to their bank account or PayPal in local currency.
